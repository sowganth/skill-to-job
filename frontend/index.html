Core Features

Engineered for College Student Success

Skill2Job eliminates the uncertainty in campus recruitment by providing quantitative skills-matching and tailored growth roadmaps.

Intelligent Skill Matching

Our weighted matching engine compares your technical and soft skills against contemporary industry job specifications to calculate your role alignment.

Detailed Skill Gap Analysis

Never guess what recruiters are looking for. Instantly see which essential skills you already possess and which specific gaps you need to bridge.

Curated Learning Pathways

Direct access to verified official documentation, tutorials, and zero-cost courses from Oracle, Spring.io, MDN, and GitHub to master missing skills.

Campus & Entry-Level Focus

Designed specifically for engineering and MCA students aiming for campus recruitment, summer internships, and graduate development roles.

Student Privacy & Security

Your college records and authentication credentials are strictly protected. Plaintext passwords are never saved in client browser storage.

Spring Boot & MySQL Architecture

Robust enterprise backend powered by Java Spring Boot REST controllers, JPA repository layers, and transactional MySQL data integrity.

How Skill2Job Works

Follow four straightforward steps to transition from academic learning to placement-ready competence.

1

Build Your Profile

Sign up with your college email and configure your current branch, year of study, and career ambitions.

2

Log Your Skills

Add technical languages, frameworks, databases, and soft skills with self-assessed proficiency levels.

3

Review Matches

Explore recommended vacancies filtered by match percentage and inspect missing prerequisites.

4

Close the Gap

Study verified learning references, update your proficiency, and watch your role alignment score rise.
